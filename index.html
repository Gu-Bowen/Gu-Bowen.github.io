About个人简介

I am a Master's Supervisor with the School of Computer Science and Technology, Xinjiang University. I received my Ph.D. in Electronic Information Technology from Macau University of Science and Technology in 2024 and my M.Eng. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2021. My research interests include wireless communications, green IoT, mobile edge computing, reconfigurable antennas, and integrated sensing and communications.

RESEARCH研究方向

Research Directions研究方向

My work centers on energy-efficient, spectrum-efficient, and intelligent wireless systems, with emphasis on low-power connectivity and active–passive coexistence.

围绕高能效、高频谱效率与智能无线系统开展研究，重点关注低功耗连接与主被动通信协同共存。

01

Backscatter Communications & Green IoT反向散射通信与绿色物联网

Constructive interference, energy recycling, wireless-powered backscatter networks, reliable low-power access, and interference/fading mitigation.

构造性干扰、能量回收、无线供能反向散射网络、低功耗可靠接入以及干扰与衰落抑制。

02

Integrated Active–Passive Communications主被动融合通信

Hybrid active–passive multiple access, cooperative transmission, coexistence mechanisms, and joint design of active and passive radio links.

混合主被动多址接入、协同传输、共存机制以及主动与被动无线链路的联合设计。

03

Wireless Edge Computing无线边缘计算

Computation offloading, wireless-powered MEC, cooperative energy recycling, and task–energy co-design for low-power edge networks.

计算卸载、无线供能移动边缘计算、协作能量回收以及面向低功耗边缘网络的任务—能量协同设计。

04

Reconfigurable & Intelligent Wireless Systems可重构与智能无线系统

RIS-assisted communications, movable and reconfigurable antennas, spectrum sensing, robust resource allocation, and intelligent radio environments.

RIS辅助通信、可移动与可重构天线、频谱感知、鲁棒资源分配与智能无线环境。

05

Integrated Sensing & Communications通感一体化

Sensing-assisted wireless communications and integrated sensing–communication design for future intelligent networks.

面向未来智能网络的感知辅助无线通信与通信感知融合设计。

Students & Openings学生与招生

I welcome motivated M.Sc. students interested in wireless communications, information processing, and related emerging research directions.

面向校内外招收通信与信息系统、电子信息等相关方向硕士研究生，欢迎对无线通信、信息处理及相关前沿研究方向感兴趣的同学联系。

Students with self-motivation, active learning ability, responsibility, solid mathematical foundations, and a willingness to engage deeply in research are particularly welcome. I value responsible, individualized, and continuous supervision, and hope to build a collaborative relationship in which both supervisor and students are fully engaged.

希望你具备较强的自驱力、主动学习能力和责任心，具有较为扎实的数学基础和良好的逻辑思维能力，并愿意持续投入科研。科研基础并非最重要，更看重持续学习、执行力、主动思考与积极沟通。在学生培养方面，重视认真负责、因材施教和全过程指导，希望师生彼此认真、共同投入、相互成就。

Prospective students are encouraged to email a CV and undergraduate transcript to bwgu@xju.edu.cn before applying.有意加入课题组的同学，请在报考前将个人简历和本科成绩单发送至 bwgu@xju.edu.cn。
