PROFILE

About

I am a Master's Supervisor at Xinjiang University and an A-category introduced talent in the School of Computer Science and Technology. I received my Ph.D. in Electronic Information Technology from Macau University of Science and Technology in 2024 and my M.Eng. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2021. My research spans wireless communications, green IoT, mobile edge computing, reconfigurable antennas, and integrated sensing and communications.

RESEARCH

Research Directions

My work focuses on energy-efficient, spectrum-efficient, and intelligent wireless systems, with a particular emphasis on active–passive coexistence and emerging network architectures.

01

Backscatter Communications & Green IoT

Hybrid active–passive communications, constructive interference, energy recycling, wireless-powered backscatter networks, and reliable low-power access.

02

Mobile Edge Computing

Computation offloading, wireless-powered MEC, cooperative energy recycling, and task–energy co-design for low-power edge networks.

03

Reconfigurable Wireless Systems

RIS-assisted communications, movable and reconfigurable antennas, spectrum sensing, robust resource allocation, and intelligent radio environments.

04

Integrated Sensing & Communications

Sensing-assisted wireless communications and integrated sensing–communication design for future intelligent networks.

JOIN THE GROUP

Prospective Students

I welcome motivated M.Sc. students interested in wireless communications, information processing, and related emerging research directions.

Students with strong self-motivation, active learning ability, responsibility, sound mathematical foundations, and a willingness to invest sustained effort in research are particularly welcome. I value responsible, individualized, and continuous supervision, and hope to build a collaborative relationship in which both supervisor and students are fully engaged.

Prospective students are encouraged to email a CV and undergraduate transcript to bwgu@xju.edu.cn before applying.
